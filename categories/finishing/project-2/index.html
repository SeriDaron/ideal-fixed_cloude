Комендантский проспект

This industrial-style luxury living room uses concrete for an unfinished look. Its modern, minimalist, and even somewhat rustic qualities, allow the concrete effect to shine, either as a focal point or subtle background. Working well with the concrete effect are the soft textures of the space. Large windows with views to the pool and garden give a sense of peacefulness light and connection with nature.
